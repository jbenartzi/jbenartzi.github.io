Syllabus


  1. High-School Math Review
  2. Motivational Examples
  3. Basic Notions:
    • Sets
    • Elements of mathematical logic
    • Sets of numbers
    • Cartesian product
    • Relations in the Cartesian plane
    • Factorials and binomial coefficients
  4. Sequences:
    • Subsequences
  5. Functions:
    • Definitions and examples
    • Range and pre-image
    • Surjectivity, injectivity, and invertibility
    • Monotone functions
    • Composition of functions
    • Elementary functions (powers; polynomials and rational functions; exponential and logarithmic functions; trigonometric functions and their inverses; hyperbolic functions)
  6. Limits and continuity:
    • Neighborhoods
    • Limits of sequences
    • Sequences and closed intervals
    • Limits of functions (limits at infinity, finite limits and continuity, points of discontinuity, limits of monotone functions)
  7. Properties and computation of limits:
    • Uniqueness of the limit and local sign of a function
    • Algebra of limits
    • Comparison theorems
    • Indeterminate forms of algebraic type
    • Substitution Theorem
    • Theorems on limits of sequences
    • Fundamental limits and indeterminate forms of exponential type
  8. Series:
    • Series and their partial sums
    • Geometric series
    • The Cauchy Condensation Test and p-series
    • Necessary condition
    • Convergence tests for series with non-negative terms
    • Alternating series
    • Absolute and conditional convergence
  9. Local comparison of functions:
    • Landau symbols
    • Infinitesimal and infinite functions
    • Order and principal part of infinitesimals and infinites
    • Asymptotic behavior
  10. Global properties of continuous functions:
    • Existence of zeros: Bolzano's Theorem
    • Range of a continuous function defined on an interval
    • Invertibility of continuous functions
    • Lipschitz and uniformly continuous functions
  11. Differentiation:
    • The derivative
    • Differentiation rules
    • One-sided derivatives, corners, cusps, and vertical tangents
    • Extrema and critical points
    • The Mean Value Theorems of Rolle, Lagrange, and Cauchy
    • De l'Hôpital's Theorem
    • Monotonicity and the sign of the derivative
    • Higher-order derivatives
    • Convexity and inflection points
    • Sketching the graph of a function
  12. Taylor expansions:
    • Taylor polynomials
    • Taylor's Theorem
    • Maclaurin expansions of elementary functions
    • Operations on expansions
    • Computing limits with Taylor expansions
    • Local behavior of functions
  13. Indefinite integrals:
    • Antiderivatives and indefinite integrals
    • Linearity and integration by parts
    • Integration by substitution
    • Rational functions
  14. Definite integrals:
    • The definite integral (Cauchy integral and Riemann integral)
    • Properties of definite integrals
    • The Integral Mean Value Theorem
    • Fundamental Theorem of Integral Calculus
    • Rules of definite integration
    • Areas between graphs
    • Integrals with variable limits (the Leibniz rule)
    • Applications to Taylor expansions
  15. Improper integrals:
    • Integrals over unbounded intervals (type I)
    • Integrals of unbounded functions (type II)
    • The Integral Test
  16. Complex Numbers:
    • Algebraic operations
    • Cartesian coordinates
    • Trigonometric and exponential form
    • Powers and n-th roots
    • Algebraic equations and the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra
  17. Ordinary differential equations (ODEs):
    • General definitions
    • First-order differential equations in normal form
    • Second-order ODEs (the pendulum; linear homogeneous ODEs with constant coefficients)
